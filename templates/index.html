What It Does

Everything You Need for
Vehicle Intelligence

A complete pipeline — from raw image to verified registration details — powered by state-of-the-art AI.

Vehicle Type Classification

Our convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on thousands of vehicle images distinguishes trucks from non-trucks with high confidence. Useful for toll, logistics, and compliance checks.

Number Plate Detection

Using Haar Cascade for plate localisation combined with EasyOCR, the system reads number plates even in varying lighting and angles, then cleans and validates the extracted text.

Registration Database Lookup

Detected plate numbers are fuzzy-matched against a dataset of 1 million+ Telangana vehicle registrations, returning owner, maker, model, fuel type, engine CC, and validity date.

Instant Verification Result

Results are colour-coded green for verified matches and red for unregistered plates — giving security personnel an at-a-glance decision in real time.

Process

How It Works

Four simple steps from photo to verified result — no technical knowledge required.

1
Upload Image

Drag & drop or select any photo of a vehicle from your device. Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, and BMP formats up to 16 MB.

2
AI Classifies

The CNN model analyses the image and classifies the vehicle as a truck or non-truck, displaying confidence percentage.

3
OCR Reads Plate

Haar Cascade detects the number plate region, then EasyOCR extracts and cleans the alphanumeric characters.

4
Database Verified

The plate is fuzzy-matched against Telangana's registration database and full vehicle details are returned instantly.
